By Air

The venue sits at Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD) — step off your flight and you're practically there.

By Train

Hyderabad Deccan (Nampally) & Secunderabad Junction connect the city nationwide; cabs reach the venue in ~45 min.

By Road & Cab

Uber & Ola are everywhere in Hyderabad. The Outer Ring Road links the airport to the whole city.

Deccan Heritage at Your Doorstep

Between sessions, step into 400 years of Qutb Shahi splendour. Hyderabad wears its history in stone, pearls and minarets.

Charminar — the four-minaret icon of the old city Golconda Fort — citadel of diamonds & echoes Qutb Shahi Tombs — the Deccan's royal necropolis Laad Bazaar — bangles, pearls & old-city bustle

The currency is the Indian Rupee (₹); cards and UPI are widely accepted across the city. International visitors should check the Indian e-Visa portal well ahead of travel, and carry a passport valid for at least six months.
